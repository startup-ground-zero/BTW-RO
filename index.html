About BMW TechWorks Romania

We are a leading software development center in Cluj-Napoca, part of the NTT DATA Group, dedicated to creating innovative solutions for the automotive industry.

Our Mission

To revolutionize automotive technology through cutting-edge software engineering, cloud solutions, and data-driven innovation. We empower talented engineers to push boundaries and create the mobility solutions of tomorrow.

Innovation First

We foster a culture centered around intellectual challenges and ambitious talent. Our team works on groundbreaking projects that directly impact BMW's global digital transformation and next-generation vehicles.

Global Impact

As part of NTT DATA and the BMW Group, our work in Cluj-Napoca influences automotive technology worldwide. We collaborate with international teams to deliver enterprise-scale solutions.
